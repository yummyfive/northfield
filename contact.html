02 / Before you enquire

A few useful things to know.

Do I need a full brief?

No. A rough idea of what you need, what your business does and when you need it is enough to start a conversation.

Can I ask for just one design?

Yes. Projects can be focused on a single piece of design, such as a flyer, menu, poster, social campaign or price list.

Do you arrange printing?

Design and production are separate unless expressly included in your project. We can prepare artwork for the agreed production requirements.

What happens after I enquire?

We’ll review the information you send and use it to understand the project, scope the work and discuss the next steps.
