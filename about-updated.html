About Northfield Design

Good design should make a difference.

We help independent businesses communicate clearly, look professional and give people a reason to remember them.

Independent by design.

Northfield Design is a small graphic design studio based in Bristol, UK.

01 / The studio

Northfield Design exists to make good graphic design more accessible to independent businesses.

From a new identity to a menu, flyer or social campaign, we focus on making every piece of communication clear, considered and useful.

We keep things straightforward: understand the problem, find the right visual direction, refine the details and deliver work that is ready to use.

Thoughtful design, without the unnecessary fuss.

Three things sit behind every project we work on.

01 / Clarity

Say it clearly.

Good design should make the important thing easier to understand, not hide it behind decoration.

02 / Typography

Choose with purpose.

Type, spacing, hierarchy and composition all have a job. We make those choices deliberately.

03 / Purpose

Make it useful.

A beautiful piece of design is only valuable if it works for the business and the people seeing it.

02 / Why independent

A small studio can keep things personal.

Working with an independent studio means your project does not have to disappear into a huge agency process.

We can keep communication direct, decisions focused and the work centred around what your business actually needs.

Direct communication

Keep the conversation simple and work through decisions together.

Considered work

Focus on the details that make a design feel right, not just finished.

Built around you

Projects are shaped around your business, audience and practical requirements.

No unnecessary extras

Clear deliverables and useful design rather than a pile of things you never asked for.
